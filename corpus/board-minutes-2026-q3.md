# Board of Directors: Minutes, Q3 2026 Meeting

## Attendance

All directors present. Also attending: the CEO, the CFO Dana Whitfield, and the General Counsel.

## Financial review

The CFO presented Q3 results. Annual recurring revenue ended the quarter at 61.4 million USD, up 31 percent year on year. Net revenue retention was 112 percent. Cash on hand covers 26 months at the current burn.

## Refund exceptions granted in the quarter

The CFO reported two refund exceptions on enterprise contracts in Q3 2026. Meridian Health Logistics was refunded 180,000 USD on day 52 after contract start, following a failed data integration that the company accepted was its own fault. Castellan Freight was refunded 95,000 USD on day 38 after its sponsoring executive left and the project was cancelled. Both were approved by the VP of Sales Operations and signed off by the CFO. The board asked for exceptions to be reported every quarter from now on.

## Strategic discussion

The board discussed a possible acquisition of a small route-optimization company. Management will return with a recommendation next quarter. No decision was taken.

## Resolutions

The board approved the refreshed equity pool and the FY2027 operating plan as presented.
