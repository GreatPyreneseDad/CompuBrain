# About Kestrel Ridge

Kestrel Ridge builds Ridgeline, an analytics platform for freight and logistics operators. We have about 310 people working across our Denver headquarters, our Lisbon office, and remote locations in more than a dozen countries.

## Our story

Kestrel Ridge started in 2016 when a small group of engineers and former dispatchers in Denver began building internal reporting tools for a regional trucking fleet. The fleet's operations team needed to see where capacity was going unused, which lanes lost money, and which drivers were consistently late to pickup. Spreadsheets could not keep up with the data coming off their telematics and load boards, so the team built a system that could.

Other fleets and brokers asked to use it. We spun the tool out as a product in 2018, opened our Lisbon office in 2021 to expand engineering and data science capacity, and released Ridgeline as a multi-tenant platform in 2023. We are still a privately held company.

## What Ridgeline does

Ridgeline ingests data from telematics feeds, electronic logging devices, load boards, transportation management systems, and fuel card providers. It normalizes that data into a common model and gives operators dashboards and reports on:

- Lane profitability, by origin, destination, equipment type, and customer
- Empty miles and deadhead trends by terminal or region
- On-time pickup and delivery performance, with root-cause tagging
- Driver and asset utilization, with configurable alerts
- Carrier and subcontractor scorecards for brokers and 3PLs

Ridgeline does not dispatch loads, bill customers, or make pricing decisions on its own. It shows operators what happened and what is trending, and it exports to the systems they already run.

## Who we serve

Our customers fall into three groups:

- **Trucking fleets**, typically 50 to 2,000 power units, that want to understand margin by lane and asset.
- **Freight brokers**, who need carrier performance data and lane-level rate history.
- **Third-party logistics providers**, who run mixed networks and need one view across customer accounts.

As of March 2026, Ridgeline is in production with 140 customer organizations in the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

## Where we work

Our Denver office is at our headquarters in the Ballpark neighborhood and houses most of our customer success, operations, and leadership teams. Our Lisbon office focuses on platform engineering and data science. The remaining staff work remotely, and we hold quarterly in-person offsites in Denver and Lisbon on a rotating basis. Our next offsite is scheduled for June 2026 in Lisbon.

## Our values

**Show the numbers.** We state what Ridgeline measures and how. If a metric has a known limitation, we document it in the product and in our sales conversations.

**Build for the dispatch floor.** Our users make decisions under time pressure. We favor clear, fast interfaces over features that look impressive in a demo.

**Own the data.** Customers keep ownership of their data. We don't sell it, and we don't use one customer's data to train models for another.

**Work in the open with customers.** We publish a quarterly reliability report and a public changelog, and we hold a customer advisory group that meets each month.
