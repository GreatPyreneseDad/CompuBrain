# FY2027 Operating Budget

**Confidential: Finance team only. Do not forward outside finance without approval from the VP of Finance.**

## Scope and Basis

This page covers the FY2027 operating budget for Kestrel Ridge, running January 1 through December 31, 2027. All figures are in USD. The budget is built on the FY2026 year-end actuals in the close binder and the December 2026 ARR roll-forward. It excludes capitalized software development and any Lisbon entity tax adjustments, which are tracked in separate schedules maintained by the Controller.

## Key Assumptions

**Bookings growth.** We plan for $7.6M in gross new ARR and $1.1M in expansion, against an opening ARR of $38.0M. This is 22% net ARR growth for the year, assuming no more than 6% logo churn. Pipeline coverage for H1 must be at least 3.0x quota before we release Q2 hiring.

**Gross margin.** Plan is 74% for the year. The main drivers are hosting cost per active telematics seat and the renewal of the regional data-feed contract in Q3. Quarterly plan is 72% in Q1, rising to 75% by Q4 as the new ingestion pipeline reduces compute per seat.

**Burn.** Planned net burn is $3.4M for the year, with monthly burn averaging $285K. Ending FY2027 cash is planned at $21.6M. At the planned burn rate, runway stays above 60 months. The burn figure does not include the Q2 working-capital swing from annual prepayments, which Treasury models separately.

## Contingency Triggers

These triggers are reviewed at the monthly close. When one is hit, Finance brings the action to the CFO within five business days.

1. **Bookings shortfall.** Net new ARR runs more than 15% below plan for two consecutive months. Action: freeze all open requisitions except backfills and revenue-generating roles.
2. **Margin floor.** Gross margin falls below 71% in any quarter. Action: FP&A reviews hosting spend by customer segment and reports on cost-to-serve options.
3. **Runway warning.** Projected runway drops below 36 months on a rolling six-month forecast. Action: CFO prepares a spending reduction plan for board review.
4. **Headcount overrun.** Actual headcount exceeds the quarterly plan by more than 5 people. Action: Finance and People Ops reconcile the open offers list within ten business days.

## Reporting and Owners

FP&A owns the monthly variance report, due by the 10th business day after month-end. The Controller owns the actuals reconciliation to the plan. Questions about assumptions go to the FP&A Lead; questions about headcount timing go to the People Finance Partner.
