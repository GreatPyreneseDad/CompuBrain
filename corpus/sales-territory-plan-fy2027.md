# Territory Plan FY2027

## Purpose and scope

This plan sets how account executives (AEs) in North America and EMEA divide accounts for FY2027, which fiscal year runs February 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027. It covers territory boundaries, named-account rules, inbound lead routing, and how AEs work with each other when accounts overlap. It applies to all quota-carrying sales roles and the SDR team. Partner and channel territories are covered in a separate document.

## Regional split

North America is divided into three territories, each led by one Regional Sales Director (NA):

- **NA-Central:** Texas, Oklahoma, Kansas, Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana, and the Rocky Mountain states.
- **NA-East:** All states east of the Mississippi except Florida, plus Florida.
- **NA-West:** Pacific states, Alaska, Hawaii, and all Canadian provinces.

EMEA is divided into two territories, led by the Regional Sales Director (EMEA):

- **EMEA-South:** Portugal, Spain, France, Italy, Greece, and North Africa.
- **EMEA-North:** UK, Ireland, Germany, Netherlands, Nordics, Poland, and the rest of Central and Eastern Europe.

Territory assignment is by the headquarters address of the account's operating entity, not the location of individual terminals or yards. A fleet headquartered in Dallas with yards in Ohio stays in NA-Central.

## Named accounts

A named account is any company on the FY2027 named list, maintained by RevOps and published to Salesforce on January 15, 2026. The list contains 84 accounts across both regions. Named accounts have these rules:

1. The assigned AE owns the account for the full fiscal year. Ownership does not move because of a missed quarter.
2. Named accounts with more than 500 power units, or with a total contract value above the threshold set by the VP of Sales, require a deal desk review before any proposal goes out.
3. Other AEs may not open opportunities in a named account. If an inbound lead arrives from a named account, it routes to the owner (see below).
4. Parent companies and subsidiaries are treated as one account. If a subsidiary is named, the parent's owner holds the relationship.

## Inbound lead routing

Inbound leads come from the website, the Ridgeline free trial, webinars, and partner referrals. RevOps routes them as follows:

- **Step 1:** Lead enters Salesforce. The routing rule matches company domain and headquarters country.
- **Step 2:** If the company is a named account, the lead goes to the named owner within 15 minutes.
- **Step 3:** If the company is not named, the lead goes to the SDR queue for qualification. SDRs qualify against the minimum criteria: 25 or more power units, or a freight brokerage with more than $5M in annual gross revenue.
- **Step 4:** Qualified leads go to the AE for the territory of the account's headquarters. Leads that fail qualification are returned to nurture.
- **Step 5:** If no AE is assigned within 24 hours, the lead escalates to the Regional Sales Director for that territory.

Leads that arrive from two territories (for example, a Mexican carrier with a Texas address) go to the territory of the billing contact's country.

## Rules of engagement between AEs

AEs are expected to resolve overlap directly before involving managers. The following rules apply:

- If two AEs are working the same company, the one with the earlier opportunity creation date in Salesforce holds the deal. Both AEs must log activity on the shared record.
- AEs do not contact a prospect that another AE has in active evaluation without written agreement from that AE or their Regional Sales Director.
- Shared-deal splits are decided by the Regional Sales Director, not by the AEs. Splits are recorded in Salesforce before the proposal is sent.
- Any disagreement that is not resolved within three business days goes to the Regional Sales Director, who decides within five business days.

## Territory changes and reviews

Territory changes during the fiscal year require approval from the VP of Sales and RevOps. Requests must be submitted in writing with the account name, the reason, and any open opportunity IDs. Changes take effect on the first day of the following month.

Territories are reviewed quarterly. The first review is scheduled for the week of May 4, 2026, after Q1 close. RevOps will publish account counts, pipeline by territory, and inbound volume at each review so that boundaries can be adjusted where workload is clearly unequal.
