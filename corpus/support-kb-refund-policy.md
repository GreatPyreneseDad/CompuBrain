# Refund Policy for Enterprise Customers

## The policy

If you are on an enterprise contract, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of your contract start date. Send the request from an authorized contact on your account to support or to your account manager. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days.

## After 30 days

After the 30-day window, fees already invoiced are not refundable. You can choose not to renew at the end of your term. If you believe your situation is exceptional, tell us; exception requests are routed internally to your account team, who will reply with a decision.

## Self-serve plans

Self-serve plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time. The current month is not refunded.
