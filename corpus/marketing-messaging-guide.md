# Messaging and Positioning Guide

This guide sets the language we use when we describe Kestrel Ridge and Ridgeline to customers, prospects, partners, and the press. It is for internal use by Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, Product, and anyone writing external copy. Owned by Marketing (Director of Product Marketing). Last reviewed March 2026. Questions go to the #messaging channel.

## Positioning statement

For freight and logistics operators who run on thin margins and too many spreadsheets, Ridgeline is the analytics platform that turns load, lane, and driver data into decisions a dispatcher or broker can act on the same day. Unlike general-purpose BI tools, Ridgeline is built specifically for freight: it understands deadhead miles, detention, accessorials, and carrier scorecards out of the box.

Use this statement as the basis for longer copy. Do not paraphrase it into claims we cannot support (see Proof points).

## Three value pillars

**1. Visibility across the load lifecycle.** Ridgeline connects tender, dispatch, in-transit, and settlement data into one view per load. Operators see where margin leaks, not only where revenue lands.

**2. Decisions at dispatch speed.** Alerts and recommendations appear in the tools dispatchers already use, with a median time from data arrival to visible insight of under 15 minutes for connected TMS and ELD feeds.

**3. Carrier and lane accountability.** Scorecards on on-time performance, tender acceptance, and claims rate let operators renegotiate with evidence rather than memory.

## Proof points

Use only these, and cite the source and date when you use one.

- 180+ fleet, broker, and 3PL customers in production as of February 2026 (source: Customer Success dashboard).
- Median connected-feed latency of 14 minutes, measured across Q4 2025 production tenants (source: Platform Engineering).
- Customer-reported on-time performance improvement averaging 3.1 points across 22 case-study accounts in 2025 (source: Customer Marketing; all figures are customer-reported and must be labeled as such).
- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA (source: Security).

Do not cite ROI percentages, savings totals, or "industry-leading" rankings unless a named analyst or audited study supports them. Ask Product Marketing first.

## Words we use and words we avoid

Use: freight, load, lane, carrier, dispatch, margin, operator, visibility, decision, accountable, connected data.

Avoid: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "AI-powered" as a standalone benefit (describe what the feature does instead), "seamless," "synergy," "leverage" as a verb, "unlock," "best-in-class," and "disrupt." Avoid calling customers "users" in external copy; use "operators" or "customers." Avoid "logistics platform" when "analytics platform for freight" is accurate.

## Boilerplate descriptions

**25 words:**
Kestrel Ridge builds Ridgeline, an analytics platform that helps freight operators see margin and act on it at dispatch speed.

**50 words:**
Kestrel Ridge builds Ridgeline, an analytics platform for trucking fleets, freight brokers, and third-party logistics providers. Ridgeline connects tender, dispatch, and settlement data into a single view per load, so operators can see where margin is lost and respond the same day. Kestrel Ridge is headquartered in Denver, with teams in Lisbon and remote across the US and Europe.

**100 words:**
Kestrel Ridge builds Ridgeline, an analytics platform for freight and logistics operators, including trucking fleets, freight brokers, and third-party logistics providers. Ridgeline connects data from transportation management systems and ELD feeds into one view per load, covering the full lifecycle from tender through settlement. Dispatchers and account managers receive alerts inside the tools they already use, and carrier scorecards give operators evidence for rate and service conversations. Kestrel Ridge was founded to replace spreadsheet-based freight reporting with analytics built for how the industry actually runs. The company has about 310 employees across its Denver headquarters, its Lisbon office, and remote teams, and serves more than 180 customers in production.
