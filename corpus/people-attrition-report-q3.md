# Attrition and Engagement Report - Q3 2026

**Confidential. Restricted to People team, function leads, and executive staff. Do not forward. No individual is identified in this report.**

## Summary

Total headcount closed Q3 2026 at 312, up from 304 at the end of Q2. Voluntary attrition for the quarter was 4.2% annualized, compared with 3.6% in Q2 and a trailing four-quarter average of 3.9%. Involuntary attrition was 1.0% annualized, driven mostly by two performance-related exits in Customer Operations and one role elimination in Marketing. Engagement survey participation was 91%, and the overall engagement index fell 3 points to 68. The decline is concentrated in Support Engineering and Lisbon-based Sales Development, and is covered in the sections below.

Customer Operations remains the highest-attrition function. Three of its four Q3 exits were within their first nine months. Marketing's involuntary exit was a role elimination tied to the Q3 budget reset, not performance.

## Exit interview themes

Exit interviews were completed for 7 of 8 voluntary leavers. Themes, in order of frequency:

1. **Career path clarity** (5 of 7 mentioned). Leavers said they could not see a next level in their current function.
2. **Workload during the Ridgeline 4.0 release** (4 of 7). Customer Operations and Support Engineering named on-call load as a driver.
3. **Compensation benchmarking** (3 of 7). Reference to the mid-year market review, which several felt was applied unevenly across teams.
4. **Manager feedback frequency** (2 of 7). Two leavers asked for more regular written feedback.

No exit interview raised a concern about harassment, safety, or conduct. Any such concern would be routed to the People Partner for the affected location the same day, outside this report.

## Engagement survey results

The Q3 pulse ran from August 4 to August 15, 2026. Participation was 284 of 312 (91%).

| Dimension | Q2 score | Q3 score | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall engagement index | 71 | 68 | -3 |
| Manager effectiveness | 74 | 72 | -2 |
| Growth and development | 62 | 57 | -5 |
| Workload sustainability | 65 | 59 | -6 |
| Belonging | 79 | 78 | -1 |

Growth and development and workload sustainability declined most. Both trends match the exit themes above. Belonging held steady across all locations.

## Flight-risk analysis by team

The People team scored each team on three signals: recent voluntary exits, pulse responses below 55 on workload or growth items, and open requisitions that have been unfilled for more than 60 days. Teams are rated low, moderate, or elevated. Ratings are reviewed by function leads and do not reflect individual performance.

- **Support Engineering (Engineering): elevated.** Two exits in Q3, workload score below 55 in the team's pulse.
- **Customer Onboarding (Customer Operations): elevated.** Three exits in the function, two open roles over 60 days.
- **Lisbon Sales Development (Sales and Partnerships): moderate.** Growth score below 55, no exits.
- **Data Platform (Engineering): moderate.** Flagged for workload after the August release window.
- **All other teams: low.**
