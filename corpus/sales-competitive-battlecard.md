# Competitive Battlecard: Ridgeline vs. FleetLens, Haulmetric, Cargoscope

Last reviewed: 14 April 2026. Owner: Sales Enablement. Check the current price book before quoting any competitor comparison, and do not cite competitor pricing that is more than 90 days old.

## Competitor Snapshot

- **FleetLens** sells telematics-first dashboards to fleet operators. It is strongest with owned-fleet customers who mainly want vehicle utilization and safety data.
- **Haulmetric** bundles analytics with its own load board and dispatch tools. It is strongest with freight brokers who already run their operations on its platform.
- **Cargoscope** targets 3PLs with multimodal and cross-border work, including customs and bonded warehousing. It is the most complex of the three and usually the slowest to deploy.

## Where We Win

- **Multi-source margin by lane.** Ridgeline reconciles up to six TMS and ELD sources into one margin model per lane. FleetLens covers telematics only. Haulmetric's model assumes its own load board is the system of record.
- **Detention and accessorial recovery.** Our detention module flags unbilled waiting time by shipper. In the 2025 pilot cohort (38 accounts), customers recovered an average of 2.1% of linehaul revenue.
- **Mixed fleets.** Buyers running owned trucks alongside brokered capacity get one view of cost per load regardless of source.
- **Time to first dashboard.** With a standard TMS connector, median onboarding is 11 business days. Prospects report 4 to 6 weeks for Cargoscope.

## Where We Lose

- **Dispatch-led buyers.** If the buyer wants dispatch, load matching, or a TMS in one contract, Haulmetric is the better fit. Ridgeline does not dispatch. Do not position us as a replacement.
- **Telematics-only fleets under about 50 trucks.** FleetLens is simpler and cheaper for this profile. Recommend it honestly rather than discounting into a weak deal.
- **Customs, bonded warehousing, ocean or rail multimodal.** Cargoscope covers these. Do not bid on them.
- **On-premises requirements.** Ridgeline is cloud-only as of 2026. Raise this in the first call, not in the final security review.

## Landmines to Set

Ask these early in discovery. Each one surfaces a question where we have an advantage or where a competitor's bundle creates a problem for the buyer.

- "Where does your load data live today, and can you export it in CSV or through an API without a separate fee?" Read the export terms of any bundled contract.
- "If you add a load board, does your margin view still count the true cost of brokered loads?"
- "How much detention did you bill last quarter, and how much did you leave unbilled?"
- "Who on your team would own the analytics after go-live?" Competitors that require professional services for every change often face a staffing gap here.
