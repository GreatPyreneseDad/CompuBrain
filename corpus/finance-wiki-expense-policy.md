# Finance Wiki - Expense and Procurement Policy

This page covers how Kestrel Ridge approves purchases, onboards vendors, uses corporate cards, submits expenses, and meets month-end cutoffs. It applies to all employees and contractors with spending authority. Questions go to the Finance Operations queue. Policy changes are approved by the Controller and posted here with an effective date.

## Purchase Approval Thresholds

Every purchase needs approval before the commitment is made. Approval covers purchase orders, software renewals, and any contract or order form, including those signed by a vendor's sales team.

| Amount (USD, total contract value) | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Up to $999 | Budget owner |
| $1,000 to $9,999 | Budget owner and Department Director |
| $10,000 to $49,999 | Department Director and Finance Business Partner |
| $50,000 and above | VP of the function, Finance Business Partner, and Controller |

Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a policy violation. Multi-year commitments are measured at total contract value. Any agreement that includes a data processing addendum or customer data access also needs Legal review, regardless of amount.

## Vendor Onboarding

No vendor may be paid until onboarding is complete. Target turnaround is 10 business days from a complete intake request.

1. Submit the New Vendor Request form in the Finance queue with the business justification, the budget owner, and the expected annual spend.
2. Provide a W-9 (US vendors) or W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E (non-US vendors) and a certificate of insurance if the vendor performs on site or handles customer data.
3. If the vendor will store or process Ridgeline customer data, complete the Security Questionnaire. Security will review it before onboarding proceeds.
4. Finance Operations verifies banking details by calling the vendor at a phone number already on file from an independent source. Bank changes sent by email are not accepted without this call-back.
5. Once the agreement is signed and approved under the thresholds above, Finance Operations creates the vendor record and issues the first PO.

## Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who need recurring purchasing authority, such as travel, team tools, and events. Card limits are set by the budget owner and approved by the Finance Business Partner. The standard monthly limit is $2,500. Travel leads may hold a limit up to $10,000.

Cards may not be used for:

- Personal purchases, including those later reimbursed
- Cash advances, ATM withdrawals, or gift cards
- Alcohol, except at client events approved in advance by a Department Director
- Splitting a transaction to stay under a limit or approval threshold

If a card is lost or stolen, report it to the issuing bank within 24 hours and notify Finance Operations. Card holders are personally responsible for receipts. Missing receipts must be documented on a Missing Receipt Affidavit within 3 business days of the transaction.

## Submitting Expenses

Submit all reimbursable expenses and card transaction reviews through the expense system. Each line needs:

- Date, merchant, amount, and currency
- Business purpose in one sentence
- Receipt image attached (required for any item over $25)
- Attendee names for any meal or entertainment expense

Submit expenses within 30 days of the transaction. Expenses older than 60 days require written approval from the Controller. Foreign-currency expenses are converted at the rate posted on the transaction date.

## Month-End Cutoff Dates

Expense reports and card reviews for a month must be submitted by 12:00 p.m. MT on the fourth business day of the following month. Late submissions roll to the next close period.

| Expense month | Submission deadline |
|---|---|
| January 2026 | February 5, 2026 |
| February 2026 | March 5, 2026 |
| March 2026 | April 7, 2026 (April 3 is a company holiday) |

The full 2026 calendar is maintained in the Finance shared calendar. Purchase orders for work starting in a given month should be approved by the 15th of the prior month to avoid accrual adjustments.
