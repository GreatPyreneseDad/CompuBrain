# Benefits Guide

**Audience:** All Kestrel Ridge employees (Denver, Lisbon, and remote)
**Owner:** People Operations
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Plan year:** 1 January to 31 December

This guide summarises the benefits available to full-time employees. The formal plan documents govern if anything here differs from them. Questions go to the People Operations inbox or to your Benefits Administrator.

## Health cover

Employees in Denver can choose between a PPO and an HMO plan through our carrier. Employees in Lisbon are covered by the national health scheme, with a supplementary private plan that the company pays for. Remote employees follow the plan for the country where they are contracted to work. If you are unsure which applies to you, check your offer letter or ask People Operations.

Dependants can be added to US plans at enrolment or during a qualifying life event. You have 30 days from the event to make changes.

## Retirement matching

US employees can contribute to the 401(k) from their first pay period. The company matches a percentage of your contributions up to a published cap. The current match formula is in the plan summary on the benefits portal. Matching vests on a three-year graded schedule.

Lisbon employees do not use the 401(k). Pension contributions follow Portuguese requirements, and Finance can confirm the current rates.

## Parental leave

All employees who have been with Kestrel Ridge for at least 90 days are eligible for paid parental leave. Eligible employees receive 16 weeks for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, regardless of gender. Leave can be taken within 12 months of birth, adoption, or placement.

Give People Operations at least 60 days' notice where possible. Lisbon employees should follow the statutory leave process in addition to this policy. Where statutory leave is longer, the statutory period applies.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget for courses, certifications, conferences, and books related to their role. Requests are approved by your manager before you spend the money. Unused budget does not roll over to the next plan year.

Submit receipts through the expense system within 30 days of purchase.

## Wellness stipend

Employees can claim a wellness stipend for gym memberships, fitness classes, mental health apps, or similar services. Claims are made through the benefits portal with a receipt or subscription confirmation. Stipend amounts and eligible categories are listed in the plan summary, and they can change at the start of a plan year.

## How to enrol and when

**New hires:** Enrolment opens on your start date and closes 30 days later. Enrol through the benefits portal. If you do nothing, you will not be enrolled in health cover.

**Annual open enrolment:** 2 to 20 November 2026, for the 2027 plan year. Changes made during this window take effect on 1 January 2027.

**Mid-year changes:** Only available after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, a birth, or a change in your dependants' coverage. Notify People Operations within 30 days.

**Steps:**
1. Log in to the benefits portal with your company credentials.
2. Review your plan options and your current elections.
3. Add or remove dependants as needed.
4. Set your retirement contribution rate.
5. Confirm your selections. You will receive an email confirmation within one business day.

Your Benefits Administrator can walk you through the process on request. Book a 30-minute session through the People Operations calendar.
