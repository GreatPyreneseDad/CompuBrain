# Ridgeline Pricing and Packaging Guide

Effective January 5, 2026. This guide covers list prices, tier contents, and add-on quoting for sales. It applies to new quotes and renewals dated on or after that day. Quote only from the list prices below and the quoting tool. Do not share this document with customers.

## Tiers at a Glance

All prices are USD per month, before tax, on annual billing unless noted.

| Tier | Per seat | Per connected unit | Minimums | Billing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team | $38 | $22 | 5 seats, 10 connected units | Annual only |
| Business | $64 | $29 | 15 seats, 40 connected units | Annual, or monthly at +10% |
| Enterprise | From $95 | From $36 | 100 seats, 250 connected units | Annual, custom terms |

Enterprise prices are starting points. Final pricing depends on volume, term, and integration scope, and goes through the deal desk.

## Seats and Connected Units

A **seat** is a named user with login access. Shared logins are not permitted and are billed as one seat per person.

A **connected unit** is a power unit (tractor or straight truck) that has sent telematics data to Ridgeline in the last 30 days, through an ELD feed or OEM API. Trailers are not counted. A unit that stops reporting for 30 days drops out of the count at the next billing cycle. Brokers and 3PLs with no telematics feeds of their own are billed on seats only; set connected units to zero in the quote.

## What Each Tier Includes

**Team** includes core dashboards, lane and load history for 13 months, email support during business hours, and up to 2 data integrations (ELD or TMS).

**Business** adds custom dashboards, 24-month data history, API access at 1,000 calls per day, up to 3 integrations, and priority support with a 1-business-day response target.

**Enterprise** adds single sign-on, unlimited integrations, a 4-hour response target, a named customer success manager, and a 99.9% uptime commitment. Enterprise terms are set in the order form.

## Add-Ons and How to Quote Them

| Add-on | Price | Available on |
|---|---|---|
| Extended history (36 months) | $4 per connected unit/month | Business, Enterprise |
| Additional integration connector | $750/month each | Team, Business |
| Sandbox environment | $400/month | Business |
| Dedicated onboarding package | $3,500 one-time | Business (included in Enterprise) |
| Premium support (4-hour response) | 12% of annual subscription | Business |

Add-ons are quoted as separate line items, never bundled into the base price. If a customer asks for an add-on that is not in this table, send the request to Sales Operations. Do not promise it.

## Quoting Steps

1. Confirm seat count and connected unit count with the customer. Ask for their ELD or telematics provider to verify unit counts.
2. Select the lowest tier that meets the minimums and the required integrations. Do not put a customer on a higher tier to reach a discount.
3. Select billing term. Team is annual only.
4. Add required add-ons as separate lines.
5. Generate the quote in the CPQ tool. Do not hand-edit line items or totals in documents.
6. Route any discount beyond the limits below to the deal desk before sending.

## Discount Limits

- **Team:** No discount beyond the annual term price.
- **Business:** Up to 10% for a two-year term, with no approval needed. Up to 15% requires deal desk approval.
- **Enterprise:** Up to 18% through the deal desk. Anything above that requires written sign-off from the Head of Sales.
