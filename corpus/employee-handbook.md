# Kestrel Ridge Employee Handbook

## Who we are

Kestrel Ridge builds Ridgeline, an analytics platform for freight and logistics operators. We are about 310 people across Denver, Lisbon, and remote. This handbook is the general reference for how the company works. Where a team keeps a more detailed document, this handbook points to it.

## Working hours and time off

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours, schedule your own day. Paid time off is 25 days a year plus local public holidays. Request time off in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Expenses

Travel is booked through the travel portal. Meals on travel days are reimbursed up to 75 USD a day. Anything over 500 USD needs manager approval before the spend, not after.

## Refunds on customer contracts

Enterprise contracts can be refunded in full if the customer asks within 30 days of the contract start date. After the window closes, fees already invoiced are not refundable, and the customer may instead cancel at the next renewal.

A refund outside the window is an exception. Exceptions are not decided by the account owner. They follow the approval path in the Sales Playbook, and anyone who receives an exception request should hand it to the account team the same day rather than answer it.

Self-serve plans are handled separately: they are month to month and can be cancelled at any time with no refund of the current month.

## Security basics

Use the company password manager. Turn on hardware-key sign-in for every system that supports it. Report a lost laptop to IT within one hour. Customer data never goes into personal accounts or unapproved tools.

## Compensation bands

Every role has a level from L1 to L8 and each level has a base salary band. Engineering bands for the current year: L3 118,000 to 142,000 USD; L4 145,000 to 178,000 USD; L5 180,000 to 221,000 USD; L6 225,000 to 270,000 USD. Sales bands are stated as on-target earnings with a 50/50 split: Account Executive 190,000 to 240,000 USD; Enterprise Account Executive 260,000 to 330,000 USD. The full table for all functions is in the Compensation Bands document kept by People Ops.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year, in February and August. Each review has a self-assessment, a manager assessment, and peer input from three colleagues. Ratings are calibrated across each function before they are shared.

## Who to ask

Questions about pay, level, or equity go to your manager or People Ops. Questions about a customer contract go to the account owner. Questions about this handbook go to the People Ops channel.
