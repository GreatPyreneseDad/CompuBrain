# Sales Playbook

## Purpose

This is the working reference for the sales team: how we qualify, price, close, and look after enterprise accounts. New hires should read it in their first two weeks.

## Qualification

We qualify on four things: a named operational problem, an owner with budget, a data source we can connect to within 30 days, and a decision date. A deal missing two of the four stays in stage 1.

## Discounting

Account Executives can discount list price up to 10 percent without approval. From 10 to 20 percent needs the regional director. Above 20 percent needs the VP of Sales Operations. Multi-year deals earn an extra 5 percent per added year, on top of any approved discount.

## Refunds and exceptions

The refund window on enterprise contracts is 30 days from the contract start date. Inside the window the customer gets a full refund on request, and the account owner files the request in the CRM.

Outside the window, a refund is an exception. Exceptions are approved by Priya Raman, VP of Sales Operations. Send her the account name, contract value, days since contract start, and the customer's stated reason. An exception over 50,000 USD also needs sign-off from the CFO, which Priya requests on your behalf. Do not promise the customer an outcome before the approval comes back.

## Handoff to customer success

Within five business days of signature the account owner runs a handoff call with the customer success manager. The handoff note covers the customer's stated goals, the data sources in scope, the named contacts, and anything promised during the sale.

## Renewals

Renewal conversations start 120 days before the term ends. The account owner owns the renewal number; the customer success manager owns the health score that goes into the forecast.
