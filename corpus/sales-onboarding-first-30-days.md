# Sales Onboarding: First 30 Days

## Purpose and owners

This plan covers your first four weeks as an Account Executive (AE) on the Sales team. It sets out what you should have access to, what you should do each week, and what we expect by the end of Week 4. Your Sales Manager owns the plan. The Sales Enablement Lead runs the product certification. The RevOps Analyst supports CRM setup and pipeline reporting.

Your start date is Monday, 2 March 2026.

## Week 1: Access and orientation (2 to 6 March)

Access requests go through IT Helpdesk on Day 1. Confirm you can log into each of these by Wednesday:

- Email and calendar
- CRM, with your user role set to AE
- Ridgeline demo environment, with the sandbox fleet data loaded
- Call recording and conversation intelligence tool
- Shared sales playbook and proposal templates

Spend the rest of the week on reading and listening, not selling. Complete the Ridgeline overview module, then read the last six won deals in CRM and write a one-paragraph summary of each. Your Sales Manager will review the summaries on Friday.

## Week 2: Shadowing (9 to 13 March)

You will sit in on at least 8 live calls this week: a mix of discovery calls, demos, and one renewal. Take notes in the CRM activity log, not in a personal doc, so the RevOps Analyst can use them later.

Rules for shadowing:

- You do not speak unless the AE hosting the call invites you to.
- Afterward, spend 15 minutes with the AE covering what they did differently and why.
- By Friday, post one question about a call in the #sales-onboarding channel.

## Week 3: Product certification (16 to 20 March)

Certification has two parts, and both must be passed before you run a demo without a shadow.

1. **Written module (Monday to Wednesday):** covers freight-operator data sources, including ELD feeds, TMS integrations, and FMCSA safety data, plus how Ridgeline scores lane performance. Pass mark is 85%.
2. **Demo run-through (Thursday):** you deliver a 25-minute demo to the Sales Enablement Lead, using the 3PL and fleet sandbox scenarios. The Lead scores you against the demo rubric.

If you do not pass the demo on the first attempt, you get one retake the following Monday.

## Week 4: First pipeline review (23 to 27 March)

Before Friday's review, update every opportunity you own in CRM. Each record needs a next step with a date, a named economic buyer or a stated reason one is missing, and a close date that matches the stage.

On Friday, 27 March 2026, you present your pipeline to the Sales Manager and RevOps Analyst for 45 minutes. Expect questions on:

- Deal stage and what moved it there
- Forecast category for each deal
- Where you need help from Solutions Engineering or Legal

## Acronyms used in Sales

- **3PL:** third-party logistics provider
- **ACV:** annual contract value
- **AE:** Account Executive
- **ARR:** annual recurring revenue
- **CRM:** customer relationship management system (our system of record)
- **DPA:** data processing agreement
- **ELD:** electronic logging device, a common source of fleet telematics data
- **FMCSA:** Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration
- **MQL:** marketing-qualified lead
- **MSA:** master services agreement
- **POC:** proof of concept, a time-boxed pilot before contract
- **RFP:** request for proposal
- **SDR:** Sales Development Representative, who books first meetings for AEs
- **SQL:** sales-qualified lead, meaning an SDR has confirmed need and budget interest
- **TMS:** transportation management system
