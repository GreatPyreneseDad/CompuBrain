# Handling Refund Requests: Agent Guide

## Inside the window

Confirm the requester is an authorized contact. Confirm the contract start date in the billing system. If the request is within 30 days of that date, tag the ticket `refund-in-window`, reply with the standard confirmation, and assign it to Billing. Do not ask the customer for a reason.

## Outside the window

Do not approve or decline. Tag the ticket `refund-exception`, reply that the request has been routed internally to the account team, and assign it to the account owner. Support does not decide exceptions and does not tell the customer who does.

## What not to say

Do not quote a likelihood of approval. Do not name internal approvers. Do not discuss other customers' refunds.
