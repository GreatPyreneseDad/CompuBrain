# Board Minutes - Q2 2026

**Meeting date:** July 14, 2026
**Location:** Denver office, with video link for remote directors
**Recorded by:** Corporate Secretary

## Attendance and Call to Order

Present: Board Chair, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Product Officer, two independent directors, one investor director. Absent: one independent director (excused). The Chair called the meeting to order at 9:04 a.m. Mountain Time. A quorum was confirmed.

The minutes of the Q1 2026 meeting (April 14, 2026) were approved without amendment.

## Financial Review

The Chief Financial Officer presented results for Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30).

- **ARR:** $41.6M at quarter end, up from $38.9M at March 31 and $29.4M a year earlier (41% year-over-year growth).
- **Net new ARR in Q2:** $2.7M, against a plan of $2.4M.
- **Paying accounts:** 642, up from 598 at the end of Q1.
- **Gross margin:** 79%, roughly flat with Q1 after hosting costs for the new data ingestion pipeline were included.
- **Cash and runway:** $33.8M in cash. Current net burn implies about 26 months of runway at the present rate.

Directors asked how much of the Q2 new ARR came from expansion versus new logos. The CFO said 58% came from expansion within existing accounts and will provide a split by segment (fleet, broker, 3PL) in the Q3 pack.

## Retention and Customer Metrics

- **Net revenue retention (trailing 12 months):** 112%, up from 108% in Q1.
- **Gross revenue retention:** 91%, unchanged from Q1.
- **Quarterly logo churn:** 2.8%. Most churned accounts were small fleets under 25 trucks. Two mid-market 3PL accounts churned, both citing a competing product's pricing; the CEO will follow up with both.

The Chair noted that NRR improvement came mostly from the lane-level analytics add-on, and asked management to confirm adoption before treating it as durable.

## Product Roadmap Update

The Chief Product Officer reviewed delivery against the 2026 roadmap.

- **Lane-level margin forecasting:** Generally available June 15, 2026. Thirty-one customers were using it in production by July 1.
- **Additional ELD connectors:** Two new vendor integrations shipped in May. A third is scheduled for September.
- **Broker marketplace integration:** In closed beta with six brokers. General availability is targeted for Q4 2026, contingent on the beta exit criteria (uptime above 99.5% for 60 days and no open severity-one defects).
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Report issued in May 2026 with no exceptions.

The Chief Product Officer proposed moving the predictive fuel-cost module from Q3 to Q1 2027 to free engineering capacity for the broker integration. The board discussed the tradeoff and agreed the change should be presented in the revised plan (see Resolutions).

## Sales Leadership Hiring

The CEO reported on the search for a VP of Sales, open since March 2, 2026. Eleven candidates have been interviewed. Three have reached the final round. The search firm's contract runs through August 31.

Directors asked whether the current sales leadership gap is slowing pipeline. The CEO said Q2 pipeline coverage held at 3.1x, but Q3 coverage is thinner, and the VP role is needed to own the enterprise broker motion. The board supported moving forward and asked for a recommendation by August 1. Compensation terms were not discussed in open session and are reserved for the Compensation Committee.

## Resolutions

1. **Resolved**, that the board approves the Q2 2026 financial results as presented.
2. **Resolved**, that the board approves the revised 2026 product plan, moving predictive fuel-cost to Q1 2027, subject to management's updated engineering capacity model by August 15, 2026.
3. **Resolved**, that the CEO is authorized to advance the VP of Sales search to final-round offers, with any offer to be brought to the Compensation Committee for approval.

Motions 1 and 2 carried unanimously. Motion 3 carried with one abstention (investor director, pending review of the search scope).
