# Support KB - Billing FAQ

This article answers common billing questions for Ridgeline customers. If your question is not covered here, contact Billing Support from the Help menu in Ridgeline or email billing@kestrelridge.example.

## Invoices

Ridgeline issues one invoice per billing period. Invoices are dated on the subscription anniversary date shown in your account settings. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on file and are also available under Account > Billing > Invoices.

Each invoice lists the plan name, the number of active seats, the billing period, and any applicable taxes. Invoice numbers use the format KR-YYYY-NNNNNN.

If you do not see an invoice within two business days of your billing date, check your spam folder first, then open Account > Billing > Invoices. If it is not listed there, contact Billing Support with your account ID.

## Payment methods

Ridgeline accepts ACH bank transfer, wire transfer, and major credit cards. Annual plans are invoiced by default. Monthly plans can be paid by card or ACH.

To add or change a payment method, go to Account > Billing > Payment Methods and select Add method. ACH setup requires verification of two small deposits, which usually post within three business days. Until verification is complete, the account shows as Pending and invoices are not charged automatically.

If a payment fails, we retry the charge on days 3, 7, and 14 after the failed attempt. Your account remains active during the retry period. After the final failed attempt, the account is moved to Suspended status, and data is retained for 30 days.

## Changing seat counts mid-term

You can add or remove seats at any time from Account > Subscription > Seats.

**Adding seats:** New seats take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current period, and the prorated charge appears on the next invoice.

**Removing seats:** Seat reductions take effect at the start of your next billing period. Until then, you keep the seats you paid for. Removing a seat does not delete the user's data, but the user loses access on the effective date.

Seat counts are measured as active named users on the day the billing period closes. Users who have been deactivated before that date are not counted.

## Tax documents

Ridgeline provides tax documents through the Billing section of your account.

- **Sales and use tax:** Shown as separate line items on each invoice. Tax is calculated based on the billing address on your account. If your tax status changes, send a tax exemption certificate to billing@kestrelridge.example before the next invoice date.
- **W-9:** Available on request for US customers. Request it through Billing Support.
- **Annual statement:** Available each January for the prior calendar year. It lists total charges, tax, and credits.

Ridgeline does not provide tax advice. Consult your own tax advisor about how these charges apply to your business.

## Updating billing contacts

The billing contact receives invoices, payment notices, and renewal reminders. To change it, go to Account > Billing > Contacts, select the current contact, and choose Replace. The new contact must be an active Ridgeline user on your account, or you can invite a new user from the same screen.

Changes take effect immediately. Notices sent before the change are not forwarded.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility, timing, and process are described in the separate Refund Policy article in this knowledge base. Refer to that article for any refund question. If you have already reviewed it and still have a question, contact Billing Support and include your invoice number.

## Still need help

Contact Billing Support at billing@kestrelridge.example or through the Help menu in Ridgeline. Include your account ID and invoice number when you write in. Billing Support is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Mountain Time.
