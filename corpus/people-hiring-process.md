# Hiring Process Guide

## Purpose and scope

This guide covers how Kestrel Ridge opens a requisition, runs the interview loop, records feedback, debriefs candidates, and gets offers approved. It applies to all full-time and contract hires across Denver, Lisbon, and remote roles. Recruiting coordinators and hiring managers should read the sections on requisitions and offer approval before starting any search. Questions about this guide go to People Operations.

## Opening a requisition

1. The hiring manager completes the requisition form in the applicant tracking system. Required fields: job title, level, team, location type (Denver, Lisbon, or remote, with country list for remote), reporting line, and a one-paragraph role summary.
2. The budget owner (the function's VP or Director) approves the headcount. Finance Business Partner confirms the seat is in the current plan. Requisitions for seats not in plan go to the CFO.
3. People Operations assigns a Talent Partner within two business days and confirms the level against the leveling framework. If the level is disputed, the Head of People decides.
4. The Talent Partner and hiring manager agree on the scorecard (see below) and the interview loop before the job is posted.

A requisition is not open until it has an assigned Talent Partner and a finalized scorecard. Postings created before that point get pulled.

## Interview loop structure

Standard loops have five stages, completed within 21 calendar days of the first screen:

- **Recruiter screen** (30 minutes): Recruiter. Confirms logistics, work authorization, and core experience.
- **Hiring manager screen** (45 minutes): Hiring Manager. Covers role fit and the candidate's most relevant work.
- **Technical or work sample** (60 to 90 minutes): Senior IC or peer from the team. For Ridgeline engineering roles, candidates work through a data pipeline exercise. For operations roles, candidates review a sample freight dataset and write up findings.
- **Panel** (3 interviews, 45 minutes each): Three interviewers from different functions. Each interviewer owns one competency from the scorecard.
- **Values and collaboration** (30 minutes): Any interviewer not already on the loop. Optional for contract roles under three months.

Interviewers must submit scorecard feedback within 24 hours of their session. Recruiters send reminders at 12 hours.

## Scorecards

Every role has a scorecard with four to six competencies. Each competency has a written definition, two or three interview questions, and a rating scale from 1 (strong no) to 4 (strong yes). Interviewers record evidence, not impressions. A rating without at least one specific example from the interview is returned to the interviewer for revision.

Scorecards are locked once the first candidate enters the loop. Changes after that point require approval from the Talent Partner and are recorded in the requisition history.

## Debrief

The debrief is held within 48 hours of the final panel interview. The Talent Partner chairs. Attendees are the hiring manager, all interviewers, and the recruiter.

Order of discussion:

1. The Talent Partner reads out each interviewer's ratings without comment.
2. Interviewers who rated 1 or 2 on any competency speak first, then those who rated 3 or 4.
3. The group reaches a recommendation: hire, no hire, or additional signal needed. Additional signal requires a named interviewer and a date no more than five business days out.
4. The hiring manager records the decision in the tracking system. The decision and its reasoning are visible to People Operations.

Debriefs do not vote. If there is no consensus, the hiring manager decides and notes the disagreement.

## Offer approval by role

Offers follow the approved band for the level. Any offer outside the band needs approval before it goes to the candidate.

- **Individual contributor, levels 1 to 3:** Hiring manager proposes, Talent Partner reviews, VP of the function approves.
- **Individual contributor, levels 4 and 5, and people managers:** Hiring manager proposes, Talent Partner reviews, VP of the function and Finance Business Partner approve.
- **Director and above:** VP of the function proposes, Head of People reviews, CFO and CEO approve.

Approvals are logged in the tracking system. Recruiters send the written offer only after the final approval is recorded. Verbal offers before approval are not permitted, including in the hiring manager's own conversations with candidates.

## Timelines and records

Target time from requisition approval to accepted offer is 60 days for individual contributor roles and 90 days for director and above. Talent Partners review open requisitions weekly with each hiring manager.

Candidate records, scorecards, and debrief notes are retained for the period set by People Operations policy. Access is limited to the hiring team and People Operations. Do not forward candidate materials outside the company or store them in personal drives.
