# Legal - Contracting Guide for Non-Lawyers

**Effective:** March 1, 2026
**Owner:** Legal Operations
**Applies to:** All Kestrel Ridge employees, contractors, and anyone who negotiates with customers, vendors, partners, or resellers

This guide explains when you need Legal involved in a contract, who is allowed to sign what, and how to get an NDA. It is written for people outside Legal. If something here conflicts with a signed agreement or a direct instruction from the General Counsel, follow the signed agreement or the instruction and tell Legal.

## When a contract needs Legal review

Send any agreement to Legal before you share it with a counterparty or sign it if it does any of the following:

- Creates a payment obligation over $25,000 total or over $10,000 in any single year
- Includes a term longer than 12 months, or any auto-renewal
- Gives a customer or partner access to Ridgeline source code, APIs beyond the public documentation, or customer data
- Involves processing personal data, including driver, dispatcher, or shipper contact information
- Is a reseller, referral, white-label, or revenue-share deal
- Comes from a customer's paper, a vendor's paper, or any form we did not write
- Changes any standard term in the Kestrel Ridge MSA, DPA, or order form

If you are unsure, send it. Legal would rather see a five-page order form that turns out to be fine than find out about an unreviewed commitment later.

## Who can sign what

Only the people listed below may sign. Signing authority is tied to role, not seniority, and it does not transfer informally.

| Document type | Who may sign |
|---|---|
| Standard order form within approved pricing, under $25,000 total, no non-standard terms | Account Executive |
| Standard order form $25,000 to $150,000 total | VP of Sales |
| Anything above $150,000, or any non-standard terms | CFO, after Legal sign-off |
| NDAs (mutual or one-way, using the approved template) | Any Account Executive, Partnerships Manager, or People Ops lead for hiring-related NDAs |
| Vendor agreements and purchase orders under $50,000 | Department Director |
| Vendor agreements over $50,000 | CFO, after Legal sign-off |
| DPAs, security addenda, and BAAs | General Counsel only |

No one signs on behalf of the company in a personal capacity, through an e-signature account registered to someone else, or by replying to an email with "approved."

## Requesting an NDA

1. Check whether the approved mutual NDA template covers your situation. Most sales and partner conversations do.
2. If it does, send the template through the e-signature tool from the Legal folder in the shared drive. Do not edit the template.
3. If the counterparty insists on their own NDA, submit it to the Legal intake queue (legal-intake@kestrelridge.example) with the counterparty name, the business purpose, and the expected duration of discussions.
4. Do not share any Ridgeline technical details, pricing models, or customer names until the NDA is fully signed by both parties.

## Turnaround times

These are business days from the time a complete request reaches the Legal intake queue. A request is complete when it includes the draft, the counterparty name, the deal value, and the target signing date.

| Request | Target turnaround |
|---|---|
| Standard NDA, counterparty uses our template | 1 business day |
| Counterparty NDA review | 2 business days |
| Standard order form with approved redlines | 3 business days |
| Non-standard order form or new reseller agreement | 7 business days |
| DPA, security addendum, or BAA | 10 business days |

Mark requests urgent only when a signing deadline is contractually fixed. Urgent requests are reviewed within one business day but must be approved by a Director or above.

## Terms you never agree to without Legal

Do not agree to any of the following in writing, on a call, or in a redline, without prior written approval from Legal:

- Unlimited liability, or liability caps above 12 months of fees
- Indemnification of the customer for anything beyond third-party IP claims
- Exclusivity, non-compete, or most-favored-customer pricing
- Source code escrow or on-premises deployment of Ridgeline
- Service level credits above the standard schedule in the order form
- Governing law or venue outside Colorado, unless the counterparty is in an EU jurisdiction and the Lisbon template is used
- Obligations to store or transfer personal data outside approved regions
- Automatic renewal with notice periods shorter than 60 days
- Any clause that lets the counterparty terminate for convenience without a notice period

If a counterparty asks for one of these, say "I'll check with our legal team" and send the request to intake. Do not offer a compromise.

## Questions

Contact Legal Operations in the #legal-help channel for process questions. For a specific deal, use the intake queue so the request is tracked. Legal Operations reviews this guide every six months; send suggested changes to the same address.
