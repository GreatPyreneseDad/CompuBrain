# Legal Memo: Refund Terms in the Enterprise MSA

Privileged and confidential. Prepared by the Office of General Counsel.

## Summary

The standard enterprise Master Services Agreement was revised in March 2026. Section 8.2 now gives enterprise customers a refund window of 45 days from the contract start date. The earlier template gave 30 days. Every enterprise contract signed on the March 2026 template or later carries the 45-day window.

## Inconsistency with internal materials

The Sales Playbook and the Employee Handbook still state a 30-day window. Those documents have not been updated since the template changed. Where the signed contract says 45 days, the contract governs, and a refund request made on day 31 through day 45 is a contractual right, not an exception. Treating such a request as an exception, or refusing it, exposes the company to a breach claim.

## Recommendation

Update the Sales Playbook, the Employee Handbook, and the public support article to 45 days for contracts on the current template. Until that is done, account owners should check the signed contract's Section 8.2 before telling a customer which window applies. Contracts signed before March 2026 keep the 30-day window unless amended.

## Open items

Legal is reviewing whether the two refund exceptions granted last quarter were in fact inside the 45-day contractual window, which would mean no exception approval was required for them.
