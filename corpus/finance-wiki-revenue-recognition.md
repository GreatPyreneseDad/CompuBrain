# Revenue Recognition for Enterprise Contracts

## Policy

Enterprise subscription revenue is recognized ratably over the contract term. Recognition does not begin on the contract start date. Because the customer holds a refund right at the start of the term, revenue for an enterprise contract is deferred until the refund window lapses, and the first period then catches up for the days already elapsed.

## Refund reserve

Finance carries a refund liability reserve equal to 2 percent of enterprise bookings in the trailing quarter. The reserve is released as windows lapse. A refund granted after the window, as an approved exception, is booked as a reversal of recognized revenue in the period the exception is approved, not against the reserve.

## Effect of the window length

The deferral period follows the window in the signed contract. If the window on current contracts is longer than the 30 days assumed in the close checklist, revenue for those contracts is being recognized too early. The controller should confirm the window length with Legal before the next quarter close.

## Close checklist

On business day 2 of each close, the revenue accountant lists contracts whose windows lapsed in the period, releases the matching reserve, and posts the catch-up entry. Exceptions approved in the period are listed separately for the CFO's review.
