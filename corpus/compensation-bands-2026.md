# Compensation Bands 2026

Confidential to People Ops and managers with a need to know.

## How bands work

Each level has a base salary band with a minimum, midpoint, and maximum. New hires are normally placed between the minimum and the midpoint. Movement above the midpoint follows sustained performance at level.

## Engineering

L3: 118,000 to 142,000 USD. L4: 145,000 to 178,000 USD. L5: 180,000 to 221,000 USD. L6: 225,000 to 270,000 USD. L7: 275,000 to 335,000 USD.

## Sales

Bands are on-target earnings with a 50/50 base and variable split. Sales Development Representative: 85,000 to 105,000 USD. Account Executive: 190,000 to 240,000 USD. Enterprise Account Executive: 260,000 to 330,000 USD. Regional Director: 340,000 to 420,000 USD.

## Customer success and support

Support Specialist: 62,000 to 78,000 USD. Senior Support Specialist: 80,000 to 98,000 USD. Customer Success Manager: 105,000 to 135,000 USD.

## Equity

New-hire equity grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Refresh grants are decided in the August review cycle.

## Geographic adjustment

Bands above are for the United States. Lisbon bands are set at 70 percent of the US figure, paid in euros at the rate fixed each January.
