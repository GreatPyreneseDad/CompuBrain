# Order Form: Halcyon Freight Lines

## Deal summary

Halcyon Freight Lines, a regional truckload carrier with 640 power units, signed an Enterprise tier contract on 12 October 2026. Annual contract value is 240,000 USD on a two-year term. The contract start date is 15 October 2026. The account owner is the Enterprise Account Executive for the Mountain West territory. The deal closed at 14 percent off list, approved by the regional director.

## Contract terms

The contract is on the standard enterprise Master Services Agreement, March 2026 template, with no amendments. Payment is annual in advance, net 30. The customer's sponsor is their VP of Operations. Procurement asked about refunds during negotiation and was told the standard refund window applies.

## Promised during the sale

Three things were promised and must be honoured: a custom detention-time dashboard delivered within 30 days of the start date; a live connector to their Samsara telematics feed in the first two weeks; and a quarterly business review with their VP of Operations.

## Onboarding handoff
<!-- scope: support -->

New enterprise customer: Halcyon Freight Lines. Go-live target is 29 October 2026. Their workspace administrator is the Director of Fleet Systems. They require single sign-on through Okta before any user is invited. Data sources in scope: Samsara telematics, McLeod transportation management system, and a nightly fuel-card file. Expect about 45 users in the first month. They have asked for a named support contact during the first 60 days.
